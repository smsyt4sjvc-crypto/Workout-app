EXM-3700LP routine · Weekly plan (G9S, Oct 2026)

Note: Supersets (A1/A2): do one set of A1, go straight to one set of A2, rest 60–90 sec, repeat. Circuits (A1–A4) list their own rest.
Note: Pick a weight where the last 2 reps of each set are hard with clean form. Go lighter in week 1 while you learn the setups.
Note: Progression: when you hit the top of the rep range on every set with clean form, add weight next session. The dumbbell tops out at 25 lb: when a DB move gets easy, slow the lowering to 3 sec and add a 1-sec pause before adding reps.
Note: Progress cardio by speed, incline or bike resistance at the same RPE rather than adding time, so you stay inside the hour.
Note: Every 6–8 weeks, take an easy week: same exercises, about half the sets.
Note: Safety: inspect the cables before every session, and check that pop pins and adjustment points are in place and tight.

## Mon · Full Body A + bike sprints
Warm-up: 7 min: bike easy (RPE 3–4) 5 min, band pull-aparts ×15 (light band), bodyweight squats ×10, arm circles ×10 each direction
A1 Leg Press: 3 × 8–12 | Back pad set so knees are at 90°; don't lock out knees
A2 Calf Press: 3 × 12–15 | Same station: balls of feet on bottom of the press plate, re-pin
B1 Chest Press: 3 × 8–12 | Press Arm in Chest Press position, back pad flat
B2 DB Single-Arm Row: 3 × 10–12/arm | Free hand braced on the seat pad, staggered stance
C1 Lat Pull Down: 3 × 8–12 | Lat Bar on high pulley; pull to upper chest, never toward head or neck
C2 DB Goblet Reverse Lunge: 3 × 8–10/leg | DB held at chest
D1 Triceps Press Down: 2 × 12–15 | Straight Bar and chain on high pulley; upper arms locked at sides
D2 DB Hammer Curl: 2 × 10–12/arm | Palm facing in
Cardio: Bike sprints, 10 min: 2 min easy, then 6 rounds of 20 sec hard (RPE 9) / 40 sec easy, then 2 min easy
Cool-down: Stretch 5 min, holding each 20–30 sec, slow and no bouncing: chest, lats, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, calves

## Tue · Zone 2 treadmill + core
Warm-up: Treadmill easy (RPE 3–4), 5 min
Cardio: Treadmill Zone 2, 35 min at RPE 5–6: brisk incline walk or easy jog
Note: Core circuit, 3 rounds (~13 min): A1 to A4 back to back, 60 sec rest between rounds
A1 Resistance Ab Crunch: 3 × 12–15 | Tricep/Ab Strap on mid pulley; Pec Dec Arms moved out of the way
A2 Dead Bug: 3 × 8/side | Bodyweight; lower back pressed to the floor
A3 Band Face Pull: 3 × 15 | Light band anchored at face height; pull toward forehead, elbows high
A4 Plank: 3 × 30–45 sec | Forearms down, body in a straight line
Cool-down: Stretch 5 min, holding each 20–30 sec, slow and no bouncing: chest, lats, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, calves

## Wed · Full Body B + incline walk
Warm-up: 7 min: bike easy (RPE 3–4) 5 min, band pull-aparts ×15 (light band), bodyweight squats ×10, arm circles ×10 each direction
A1 Chest Supported Mid Row: 3 × 8–12 | Chest flat on pad; pull handles until even with midsection
A2 DB Goblet Squat: 3 × 12–15 | DB at chest; 3-sec lowering
B1 Incline Press: 3 × 8–12 | Press Arm in Incline Press position, back pad extended
B2 DB Romanian Deadlift: 3 × 12–15 | DB held by both ends; hinge at hips, soft knees, 3-sec lowering
C1 Standing Leg Curl: 3 × 10–12/leg | Heel under bottom leg pad, knee slightly below top roller pad
C2 Band Pallof Press: 3 × 10/side | Band anchored at chest height; press straight out, resist the twist
D1 Biceps Curl: 2 × 10–12 | Straight Bar on low pulley; upper arms locked at sides
D2 DB Overhead Triceps Extension: 2 × 12–15 | Both hands on the DB, elbows pointing up
Cardio: Treadmill incline walk, 10 min: steady at RPE 6, a brisk walk at roughly 8–12% incline
Cool-down: Stretch 5 min, holding each 20–30 sec, slow and no bouncing: chest, lats, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, calves

## Thu · Bike intervals + core
Warm-up: Bike, 8 min, building from easy to moderate
Cardio: 4×4 intervals, 28 min: 4 min hard (RPE 8) / 3 min easy (RPE 3), × 4. In weeks 1–2, do 3 rounds.
Note: Core circuit, 3 rounds (~14 min): A1 to A4 back to back, 60 sec rest between rounds
A1 Oblique Crunch: 3 × 10/side | Tricep/Ab Strap on mid pulley, Press Arm in Storage position; alternate left and right
A2 Side Plank: 3 × 30 sec/side | Hips lifted, body in a straight line
A3 Bird Dog: 3 × 8/side | Opposite arm and leg; 2-sec hold at the top
A4 DB Suitcase March: 3 × 30 sec/side | DB in one hand at your side; march in place without leaning
Cool-down: Stretch 5 min, holding each 20–30 sec, slow and no bouncing: chest, lats, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, calves

## Fri · Full Body C + bike tempo
Warm-up: 7 min: bike easy (RPE 3–4) 5 min, band pull-aparts ×15 (light band), bodyweight squats ×10, arm circles ×10 each direction
A1 Standing Shoulder Press: 3 × 8–12 | Press Arm in Standing Shoulder Press position; stand facing into the machine
A2 DB Goblet Split Squat: 3 × 8–10/leg | DB at chest; back knee drops toward the floor
B1 Pectoral Fly: 3 × 10–12 | Seat set so handles are about shoulder level, arms parallel to floor
B2 DB Single-Leg RDL: 3 × 10/leg | DB in the hand opposite the working leg; free hand on the frame for balance
C1 Lat Pull Down: 3 × 10–12 | Same setup as Monday: Lat Bar on high pulley; pull to upper chest, never toward head or neck
C2 DB Lateral Raise: 3 × 12–15/arm | Free hand on the frame; raise to shoulder height
D1 Triceps Extension: 2 × 12–15 | Tricep/Ab Strap on mid pulley, Pec Dec Arms out of the way; sit facing away, torso at about 45°
D2 Band Face Pull: 2 × 15 | Light band anchored at face height
Cardio: Bike tempo, 10 min: steady at RPE 6–7
Cool-down: Stretch 5 min, holding each 20–30 sec, slow and no bouncing: chest, lats, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, calves

## Sat · Long Zone 2 + arms & shoulders
Cardio: Bike Zone 2, 20 min at RPE 5–6 (first 3 min easy)
Cardio: Treadmill Zone 2, 20 min: brisk incline walk at RPE 5–6
Note: Arms & shoulders circuit, 3 rounds (~13 min): A1 to A4 back to back, 45–60 sec rest between rounds
A1 DB Hammer Curl: 3 × 10–12/arm | Palm facing in
A2 Band Triceps Pushdown: 3 × 15 | Band anchored high on the frame; upper arms locked at sides
A3 Band Face Pull: 3 × 15 | Light band anchored at face height
A4 Single-Leg Calf Raise (DB): 3 × 15/leg | Bodyweight or holding the DB (leave weight at 0 for bodyweight); hand on the frame for balance
Cool-down: Stretch 5 min, holding each 20–30 sec, slow and no bouncing: chest, lats, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, calves

## Sun · Rest or active recovery
Note: Full rest is fine. To move a little: 20–30 min easy bike or walk (RPE 3–4), then 10–15 min of full-body stretching with 20–30 sec holds.
